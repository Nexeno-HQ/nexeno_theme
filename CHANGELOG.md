## 1.0.0

### Added
- Nexeno Light and Dark variants, chosen per account under Personal settings → Appearance

- Layouts for Files, Photos, Dashboard, Activity, Calendar, Contacts, Deck, Notes, Tasks, Talk, Collectives, Teams, Office, Mail and the App store
- Version-specific adjustments for Nextcloud 32, 33 and 35
